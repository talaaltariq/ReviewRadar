## Datasets
- **Amazon Reviews 2023**: [https://amazon-reviews-2023.github.io/main.html](https://amazon-reviews-2023.github.io/main.html)

- **Yelp Open Dataset**: [https://business.yelp.com/data/resources/open-dataset/](https://business.yelp.com/data/resources/open-dataset/)
  - Fields utilized: `stars`, `text`

## Pretrained Models & Libraries
- **all-MiniLM-L6-v2**: [sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2)
  - Pretrained sentence transformer mapping sentences to 384-dimensional dense semantic vectors (used strictly as a frozen feature extractor).
- **scikit-learn**: [https://scikit-learn.org/](https://scikit-learn.org/)
  - `TfidfVectorizer`, `LogisticRegression`, `Pipeline`, metrics.
